    Save your passkey

    Your device asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN.

    This page is open at , so the passkey is saved by this browser, on this computer. The browser needs a passkey manager for that. On a Linux desktop that means Chrome or Chromium signed in to Google Password Manager, or a password manager extension that keeps passkeys; where the browser offers it, a phone nearby over Bluetooth also works. Firefox on Linux has no passkey manager of its own. If this browser has none, open the page from a computer that has one: run ssh -o ExitOnForwardFailure=yes -L localhost::127.0.0.1: <this computer> there (it stops, instead of only warning, when that port is already taken on that computer), then open /approve in its browser.
